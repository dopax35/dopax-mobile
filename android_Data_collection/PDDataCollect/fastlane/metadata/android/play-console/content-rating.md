# Google Play — Content Rating questionnaire (IARC)

Answer key for the IARC content-rating questionnaire. The expected
final rating for DopaX is **Everyone** in the US (ESRB) and the
equivalent for other regions, because the app contains no user-
generated content, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no violence
or fear-inducing content, and no social interaction features.

## Category to select

**Health / Medical / Lifestyle** (DopaX is a research data-collection
app, so the most accurate IARC umbrella is "Reference, news, or
educational" with the secondary category "Medical").

## Questionnaire answers

Use the rating tool with these answers. (Skip any question not asked.)

1. Does your app contain violence?  **No.**
2. Does your app contain sexual content or nudity?  **No.**
3. Does your app contain profanity or crude humor?  **No.**
4. Does your app reference, depict, or simulate use of controlled
   substances, alcohol, or tobacco?  **No.**
5. Does your app reference or depict gambling, including simulated
   gambling?  **No.**
6. Does your app contain user-generated content (UGC)?  **No.**
   *Rationale: questionnaire answers and motor-test data are
   uploaded to a private research server, not shared with other
   users; there is no chat, forum, comment, or media-share feature.*
7. Does your app share location with other users?  **No.**
8. Does your app allow users to interact or exchange content with
   each other?  **No.**
9. Does your app contain digital purchases?  **No.**
10. Does your app collect, use, or share personal information?
    **Yes.** (See the Data Safety section for the full breakdown.)

Resulting rating, all regions: **Everyone / 3+ / Suitable for all.**
